# Bounded correction and escalation

The immediate lead classifies failures: implementation, test defect, dependency,
environment, permissions, resource exhaustion, transient service, or unknown.
Use source Plan J and Clean Code 6–7 through their indexes.

Create a general correction role on a different approved model from the failed
implementer, preferably a capable economical DeepSeek/Flash-class choice.
It receives only findings, contract, applicable policy packet/ranges, changed
paths, failure evidence and required checks. Respect [permissions](12-permissions.md):
automatic creation alone never grants edits. An authorized worker applies its
proposal when correction edit authority was not included in approval.

An attempt includes hypothesis, relevant change, affected check and expected vs
observed comparison. Initial diagnosis is not an attempt. Keep failure signatures,
attempt IDs and lineage counters through all child calls and compaction.

After three failed attempts, stop mutation, preserve/quarantine evidence and
escalate. L0 may authorize one materially revised cycle for a genuinely different
approach within approved budgets: maximum six attempts per lineage. After six,
further work requires an explicitly revised plan/budget, preserving history.
Stop earlier at any tighter user/runtime limit or lack of useful progress.

Rerun failed gates and affected tests after repairs. Any changed source/config
invalidates dependent review evidence. Do not weaken assertions, remove controls,
change criteria, or retry unknown external side effects without reconciliation.
Environment failures do not justify speculative product edits.

For quarantine, read full source Plan H/M before cleanup: retain a verified
reconstructable recovery package, protect sensitive artifacts, fence owned
processes and preserve unrelated work. Escalate concise diagnostics via
[report filtering](15-report-escalation.md); do not dump transcripts into L0.
