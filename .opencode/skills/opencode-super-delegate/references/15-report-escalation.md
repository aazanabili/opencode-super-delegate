# Compact reports, escalation and final authority

Children report to their immediate parent. Parents reconcile findings and
evidence before reporting upward; do not concatenate child reports. Return stable
artifact paths/revisions so the premium owner can request exact evidence later.

Default target: leaf reports up to 250 words, lead reports up to 500 words, top
decision packet up to 800 words, plus compact structured fields. These are
soft presentation budgets: never omit a material blocker, risk, failed control,
uncertainty or permission violation to meet them. Put details in linked artifacts.

Each upward report includes:

- Task/parent/lineage/attempt and policy version; model actually used.
- Objective and requirement IDs; result/candidate identity and changed paths.
- Verified facts, finding severity, evidence references, checks and real outcomes.
- Policy coverage/exclusions, residual risk and unresolved dependencies.
- Cost/usage observed versus estimated, remaining call/depth/retry budgets.
- A recommendation and exact decision required, or `decision_required: false`.

Escalate only for material ambiguity, conflicting evidence, unavailable controls,
budget exhaustion, capability failure, out-of-scope authority, or blocked gates.
Include options with consequences and the smallest question the parent can resolve.
Routine progress stays in task-local state; L0 gets milestone or decision reports.

L0 decides final acceptance from independent lead reports and traceability to
the same integrated candidate. If reports conflict or omit evidence, request
targeted investigation by another approved reviewer; do not rubber-stamp prose.
Implementation success is not acceptance. Publication needs both L0 acceptance
and the user's existing publication authority. L0 can delegate the authorized
mechanical command while retaining the decision and requiring its actual result.
